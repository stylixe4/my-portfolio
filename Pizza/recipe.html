Recipe: Pizza Dip

Ingredients

For the Pull-Apart Bread

  • 16.3 oz can Grands biscuit dough (8 count)
  • ¼ cup melted butter
  • 2 tsp parmesan cheese, grated
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning

For the Pizza Dip

  • 8 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup ricotta cheese
  • 3 tsp Italian seasoning, divided
  • 1 cup marinara sauce
  • 1 ½ cups mozzarella cheese, grated
  • 8 slices pepperoni, quartered

Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.

2. Cut each biscuit in half, then roll each half into a ball.

3. Mix melted butter, parmesan, and 1 tsp Italian seasoning in a bowl.

4. Roll biscuit dough in butter and arrange in skillet.

5. Mix cream cheese, ricotta, and seasoning; spread in center.

6. Top with marinara sauce.

7. Top with mozzarella cheese and remaining seasoning.

8. Add pepperoni on top.

9. Bake for 25–30 minutes until golden.
