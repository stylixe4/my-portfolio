Recipe: Cinnamon Roll Wreath with Apples

Ingredients you’ll need

  • Crescent roll dough – you can use canned crescent roll dough, or sheets of canned crescent dough.
  • Granulated sugar & Brown sugar
  • Ground cinnamon
  • Unsalted butter (softened)
  • Pazazz apples (sweet, tart and extra crispy)
  • Cream cheese, Milk, Vanilla extract, Powdered sugar

Step by step instructions

  1. Roll out the crescent roll dough: Place a large piece of parchment paper on a flat work surface and press seams together.
  2. Top with the cinnamon sugar butter: Combine softened butter with sugars and cinnamon, then spread evenly.
  3. Add the apples: Core and thinly slice one Pazazz apple, place in an even layer.
  4. Roll it up: Roll the dough up tightly over the apples into a tube.
  5. Slice and Braid: Slice lengthwise, separate halves, and braid them together.
  6. Form the wreath: Bring the ends together, bake at 350°F for 20-25 minutes until golden brown.
